# Formal E2-D result summary

Status: 5,000/5,000 new rows Full Exact Certified. The 1,000-row Final E1 baseline was reused and was not solved again. All comparisons are paired on the frozen E1 sample identity.

## E2-D1 — shortage valuation

Policy counts for beta 2/4/6 respectively are:

- beta 2: P1=200, P2=26, P3a=4, P3b=1, P4=737, P5=32.
- beta 4: P1=208, P2=26, P3a=4, P3b=3, P4=759, P5=0.
- beta 6: P1=208, P2=26, P3a=4, P3b=3, P4=759, P5=0.

The beta 4 and beta 6 first-stage aggregate summaries coincide. Relative to beta 2, beta 4 raises mean Q, F reservation, R, and worst-scenario exercise expenditure. Mean physical shortage in heterogeneous service units does not fall: it is 1.79380e9, 1.80274e9, and 1.80667e9 for beta 2/4/6. This aggregate is dominated by Cracker units and must be read with the commodity-level results. The coefficient-normalized shortage value improves by about 303,848 on average from beta 2 to beta 4 (36 improved, 964 unchanged) and is unchanged from beta 4 to beta 6. The evidence therefore shows a small beta-2-to-beta-4 reallocation effect followed by a plateau, rather than a general monotone reduction in raw physical units.

T-COST rises mechanically when beta increases because the objective's shortage valuation coefficient changes; these cross-beta levels are not a common-welfare comparison. All 1,000 worst scenarios remain h09/Katrina at every beta.

## E2-D2 — commodity priority

Water priority leaves aggregate policy labels unchanged but reallocates F/R toward Water: mean Water shortage falls by 4,605,032, while mean Vaccine and Cracker shortages rise by about 55,880 and 23,528,047. Vaccine priority yields 208 P1 and 792 P4 cases, increases Vaccine Q/F/R expenditure, and lowers mean Vaccine shortage by 758,373; Water and Cracker shortage rise by about 5,794,968 and 73,115,295. Cracker priority yields 146 P1 and 854 P4 cases, increases Cracker Q/F/R expenditure, and lowers mean Cracker shortage by 83,575,253; Water and Vaccine shortage rise by about 5,794,968 and 307,205.

Thus every priority treatment improves the target commodity's physical shortage but shifts shortage to non-target commodities. The sourcing response differs by archetype: Water priority mainly adds F/R without changing Q; Vaccine priority adds Q/F/R for Vaccine; Cracker priority adds both Q and F/R for Crackers. Same-item Q/F coexistence remains zero in all cells, and cross-item mixed policies disappear under Vaccine and Cracker priority. All 1,000 worst scenarios remain h09/Katrina in every priority cell.

These are descriptive parameter-space results, not real-world probabilities or structural welfare theorems. No parameter was adjusted from observed outcomes.
